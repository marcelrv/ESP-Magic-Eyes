While this page is open, normal eye motion is paused so the servos stay exactly where the sliders put them. Motion resumes automatically about 30 s after you leave this page, or right away with “Resume motion”.

Reference poses

Move all servos at once, using the current slider values (including unsaved changes).

1. Look straight ahead

Adjust pan and tilt until both eyes look straight forward. This is the 0° point every gaze movement is measured from.

“Check” briefly moves the eyes the way a positive command would (same as dragging right/up on the manual gaze pad). If they go the wrong way, tick “Reversed” for that servo and save.

2. Close the lids — just touching

With the eyes straight ahead, move each lid until upper and lower lid just touch each other. Blinks, winks and sleep close the lids exactly to this point.

3. Open the lids

Move each lid to its fully open position. Resting and natural-mode lid positions are measured between this point and the closed point.

4. Half open — make both eyes match

Lid linkages aren’t linear and differ per eye, so with only closed and open calibrated, in-between positions (like the resting look in natural mode) can sit at different heights left and right. Move each lid to half open so the left and right eye look the same.

Compare both eyes

Sweep all four lids together through the calibrated range (current slider values, including unsaved changes). Both eyes should look the same at every position; if one is off somewhere, adjust the nearest calibration point above.

85%
5. Safety limits (advanced)

Hard limits the motion engine will never drive past. For pan/tilt they are also the ±45° ends of the gaze range. Lid limits are widened automatically on save if they don’t include the closed/open points.
